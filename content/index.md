# Léa Giezek

Fifteen years across brand, retail, agency and product - every rung of the craft before the direction. I don't systematize what I haven't practiced - and I nurture the practice and the people, not just the system.

I lead the way I learned: hands dirty first.

Having done the work myself is what tells me **when to step in and when to stay out** - close enough to help when it counts, **never close enough to hover.**

Enough engineering to earn the room.

**I read the code and ran component QA myself** - to back my team up when they needed it, and to understand the work well enough **never to decide against it.**

Direction earned, not assigned.

I've designed **the brand, the product, the system**. So when I set direction, it's grounded in the work - **I know what it costs to execute.**

## The best governance is the kind you don't have to police.

Rigor and care aren't opposites - they're the same discipline. Every principle here trades the easy version for the one that lasts: discovery before code, ethics before AI, wellbeing before delivery. **Distribute the ownership, or become the bottleneck.**

## I don't build design systems. I build experience systems.

Fifty would be padding. Three prove it - a platform, a brand, and a space you could immerse yourself in. **Same thinking, three worlds - no craft left in a silo.**

Governance

**The product failed. The platform scaled.** Same system, rebuilt from zero - into the standard a strategic digital portfolio depends on, and the community that makes it last.

Systematize a brand

Language

2,500 people, one language - before I'd have called it a design system. Then a corporate rebrand, translated for screens, and every siloed UI kit in the portfolio put on one standard. **Anything can be systematized** once you understand each craft. This is where Elements starts.
